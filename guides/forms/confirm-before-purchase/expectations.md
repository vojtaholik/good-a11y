# Expectations: confirm-before-purchase

1. Activating the one-step order or booking does not make it final at once: either a review step asks the user to confirm and confirming places it, or the page that confirms it offers a control that cancels it.
2. Backing out works: leaving the review step with its cancel or back button, or Escape, leaves nothing ordered or booked; or the cancel control on the confirmation page says the order or booking is cancelled.
