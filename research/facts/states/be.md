# Belgium (BE)

Belgium transposed the EAA in many federal, regional and community acts (EUR-Lex lists 22 national
measures for 32019L0882). This file covers the federal act that brings e-commerce and consumer banking
services into scope: Loi du 5 novembre 2023, which inserts a title 5 "Exigences en matière d'accessibilité
applicables aux services" into book VIII of the Code de droit économique (CDE).

Source law text: Justel (FPS Justice consolidated legislation database), ELI
https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/eli/loi/2023/11/05/2023046827/justel . Penalty amounts come from the
consolidated CDE itself, ELI https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/eli/loi/2013/02/28/2013A11134/justel .

Not covered here: electronic communications (law of 20 July 2023 amending the law of 13 June 2005),
audiovisual media (law of 20 July 2023 and community decrees), transport (law of 8 May 2024), e-books
(Flemish and French Community decrees), and regional self-service terminal rules. Those are listed on
EUR-Lex but were not read for this file.

Note on the EUR-Lex index: it lists the SPF Economy measure as "Belgisch Staatsblad ; Number: 2023/46360 ;
Publication date: 2023-10-01". The Moniteur belge page for numac 2023046360 of 1 October 2023 shows no text.
Justel gives numac 2023046827, published 28 November 2023, for the act cited below.
