# Finland (FI)

Finland split the transposition: products in a new act (102/2023), services (including e-commerce)
in a new chapter 3 a added to the Act on the Provision of Digital Services (306/2019) by 104/2023.

### FI-PENALTIES-1
- claim: The Act on the Provision of Digital Services (306/2019) states no fine amounts. Its enforcement tool is a conditional fine (uhkasakko) attached to a compliance order, governed by the Act on Conditional Fines; the amount is not stated in the law. Act 104/2023 did not add a fine. Value is unknown for a fixed amount.

- value: unknown (no fixed fines stated; conditional fine possible, amount not set in law)
