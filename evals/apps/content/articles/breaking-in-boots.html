Breaking in leather boots

By Petr, 28 September 2026, 5 minute read

New leather boots need about 50 kilometres before they stop fighting you. Here is how we got there without ruining a holiday.

Week one: around the house

Wear them indoors for an evening at a time. Lace them the way you will on the trail.

Week two: short walks

Five to ten kilometres on easy paths. Stop at the first hot spot and tape it.

Week three: a real hike

A full day with a light pack. If they feel fine at the end, they are ready.
