# Denmark (DK)

Source law text: Retsinformation, LOV nr 801 af 07/06/2022 (https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2022/801,
full XML at the same URL with `/xml`). Amended by LOV nr 637 af 11/06/2024
(https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2024/637), which adds guidance for microenterprises (§ 13 a),
compliance orders for products (§ 49), information access (§ 51 a) and a new Annex 3. It does not
change the scope, the supervisory bodies, the exemptions or § 57 on penalties.
