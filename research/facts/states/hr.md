# Croatia (HR)

Source law text: Narodne novine (official gazette of the Republic of Croatia), narodne-novine.nn.hr.
Base act: Zakon o zahtjevima za pristupačnost proizvoda i usluga, NN 89/2025 (13.6.2025), document 1231.
Amending act: NN 114/2026 (7.10.2026), document 1371, in force on the eighth day after publication.
The amendment renumbers Article 5 definitions (old points 10 to 39 become 14 to 43); article
references below are to the text as published in NN 89/2025 unless stated. Both acts are listed
as national transposition measures of Directive (EU) 2019/882 in the Publications Office index
(72019L0882HRV_202503432 and 72019L0882HRV_202607747).
