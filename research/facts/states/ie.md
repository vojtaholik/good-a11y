### IE-PEN-1
- claim: Failing to comply with Regulation 5(1) (providing only services that meet the accessibility requirements) is an offence under Regulation 32(1). Under Regulation 32(6) it is punishable on summary conviction by a class A fine or up to 6 months' imprisonment or both, and on indictment by a fine up to EUR 60,000 or up to 18 months' imprisonment or both. The S.I. does not state the amount of a class A fine.
- field: penalties
- value: Summary: class A fine and/or up to 6 months; indictment: up to EUR 60,000 and/or up to 18 months
- source: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2023/si/636/made/en/print
- excerpt: "(a) on summary conviction to a class A fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or to both, or (b) on conviction on indictment to a fine not exceeding €60,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 18 months or to both."
