# Standards: EN 301 549 (ETSI) and WCAG (W3C)

Researcher: research-eu. Retrieved 2026-11-03. Two EN 301 549 versions are on etsi.org:
V3.2.1 (2021-03), whose clause 9 points to WCAG 2.1, and V4.1.1 (2026-09), whose clause 9 points
to WCAG 2.2. `STD-SC-*` and `STD-EN-9.*` cover WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA with EN 301 549 V3.2.1.
`STD-SC22-*` and `STD-EN4-9.*` cover WCAG 2.2 and EN 301 549 V4.1.1. Whether either version is
cited in the Official Journal under Directive (EU) 2019/882 is recorded in `eu.md` (`EU-HS-*`).

Excerpts from the ETSI PDFs were taken from text extracted from the PDF; line breaks and
hyphenation spacing are normalised.
